## What it is
- **Siam Quantum Square (SQ²)** is Chulalongkorn University's flagship program for quantum technology research and talent development. It describes itself as Thailand's largest quantum center.
- **Website:** https://www.sq2.chula.ac.th/ (known sub-page: `/activities`)
- **Facebook:** "SQ² Siam Quantum Square at Chulalongkorn University" (profile id `61582931473011`), which is the page you asked about.
- **Location:** G floor, Samyan Mitrtown, Bangkok.
- **Launched:** Monday, 26 Jan 2026. The launch was presided over by Prof. Dr. Wilert Puriwat, President of Chulalongkorn University.

## Strategy
- **Cloud-first:** Thai researchers will use quantum hardware through cloud platforms (e.g. IBM) instead of building quantum computers in Thailand.
- Aims to make Thailand a Southeast Asian quantum hub and prepare Thai businesses for the "quantum era."
- **Education:** international Master's and PhD programs in quantum technology, plus short courses and executive programs built with IBM, the Chula Graduate School and QTFT.
- **Planned exchanges:** student and staff exchanges under discussion with KAIST (Korea) and NIMS (Japan).

## Partners
IBM Thailand · Quantum Technology Foundation (Thailand) (QTFT) · Western Digital · Siam Commercial Bank · AIST / G-QuAT (Japan) · Qunova Computing (Korea) · KAIST · NIMS
